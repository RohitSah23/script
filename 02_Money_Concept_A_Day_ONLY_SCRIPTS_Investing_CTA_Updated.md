## 09. Rule of 72: paisa double kab?

Maan lo Priya ne ₹10,000 invest kiye aur usko samajhna hai ki ye double hone mein kitna time le sakte hain. Ek quick mental trick hai Rule of 72. Agar tum 9 percent saalana return assume karo, toh 72 ko 9 se divide karo. Answer aata hai 8 years. Matlab aise return par paisa roughly 8 saal mein double ho sakta hai. Actual compound formula se time lagbhag 8.04 saal aata hai. Par ek baat yaad rakhna, 9 percent sirf assumption hai; market ka return fixed nahi hota. Tum kis assumed return par calculate karoge, 6 ya 9 percent? Comment karo. Different scenarios Trackk ke calculators par compare kar sakte ho.
