# TRACKK — Money Concept a Day | 10 Complete Ginger-Cat Storytelling Scripts

**Version:** Consolidated script master • **Status:** Client approval draft, not published • **Audience:** 18–24, students, interns, first-salary earners.

**Video identity:** Original **ginger tabby cat**, cream belly, **navy varsity jacket**, **brown sling bag**, bold flat-2D outline; one identical character across every pose. Blue background `#375CA8`; logo visible top-left on dark chip; subtitles in Roman Hinglish, highlighted arithmetic; 1080×1920, 30fps. **Target 40–55 seconds**, depending on the actual voice read; scripts must be timed in a narration test before final edit.

## Production flow

1. Hook poses a relatable **question with a number**, first 3 seconds.
2. A named person or small everyday situation creates tension. Ginger cat acts out the scenario.
3. Build the calculation **in the same order the narrator says it**, left-to-right on screen.
4. Hold the final figure 0.5 seconds; reaction should follow the result, not spoil it.
5. Ask a **specific comment question**; mention one relevant Trackk feature or investing option naturally at the end.

**Trackk rules:** Always spell **Trackk**; official icon + wordmark on screen whenever spoken/shown; no download imperative, price/return promises or specific stock buy recommendation. Show *actual* approved product screens only. Review and approval required before publication.

**Educational disclaimer:** For assumed market returns, speak *'maan lo', 'assumed, guarantee nahi'* and show the same on-screen. Mathematical hypotheticals are not financial advice. The user-facing CTA is not an instruction to invest without evaluating risk.
