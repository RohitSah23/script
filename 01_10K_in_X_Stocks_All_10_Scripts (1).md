# TRACKK — ₹10K in X Stocks | 10 Complete Storytelling Scripts

**Version:** Consolidated review master • **Status:** Editorial draft, not publication-approved • **Audience:** 18–24, students and first-salary earners • **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 30fps • **Duration target:** 45–55 seconds per reel.

## Non-negotiable production rules

- Each episode is a **what-if investment journey** from 2005 → 2015 → 2025, with a question hook, actual arithmetic, a relatable holding dilemma, and a comment prompt. Trackk is a natural investing option at the end, **not** a specific stock recommendation.
- Keep speech in complete conversational Hinglish sentences. Give each episode a distinct hook and emotional framing.
- **Numbers need independent exchange validation before publishing.** Existing chapter calculations are *draft price-only illustrative estimates* from third-party adjusted historical data, not certified exchange-level total-return calculations. Check dated adjusted closing prices, splits/bonuses, demergers, mergers, shares outstanding and each stock's corporate-action history. Do not present draft figures as verified facts.
- **Important math distinction:** The `2005 comparable/adjusted price` in the source scripts is **not necessarily the price of one physically tradable share in 2005**. Dividing ₹10,000 by that figure yields **share-equivalent units**, sometimes fractional, **not historical physical shares purchased**. Never narrate '2005 mein ek share ₹X ka tha, toh itne actual shares liye' using these adjusted figures. For a reel that shows literal share counts, rebuild each example using historical unadjusted transaction prices, whole shares purchased and every relevant corporate action. The chapter formulas are interim comparison arithmetic only.
- Values exclude dividends, demerged holdings, taxes, costs and any cash uninvested due to whole-share purchases. Especially review Reliance's demerger history. Claims of profit are simplified **price-only growth**, not actual realized profit.
- Show the formula before the result. Speak numbers that are on screen; brand name always **Trackk** and logo (icon + wordmark) visible whenever mentioned. Use real approved product UI, not fabricated screens.
- Footer: *Educational only. Past performance is not a guarantee. Not investment advice.* **Nothing goes live before client approval.**

## ₹10K in X Stocks | Episode 01 | Reliance (2005 → 2015 → 2025)

**Status:** Draft for client approval only. Do not publish.  
**Audience:** 18–24, students and first-salary earners.  
**Target:** 50–55 seconds; read aloud and trim if needed.  
**Visual format:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 30fps; near-black #0E1116, green gain and red drawdown. No ginger-cat mascot (reserved for Money Concept page).  
**Historical basis:** Year-end prices from [ChartMine historical adjusted prices](https://chartmine.ai/seasonality/reliance/), including bonus and split adjustments. Data must be independently verified against exchange records before publication.

## Hook options
1. **Recommended:** Kya tum 20 saal tak ek stock ko chhod paate?
2. "2005 mein Reliance ko ₹10,000 diye hote... 2025 mein kya milta?"
3. "2015 mein bech dete, ya 2025 tak hold karte? Reliance ka maths dekho."

## Natural conversational Hinglish voiceover (Roman)

"Kya tum 20 saal tak ek stock ko chhod paate?

Socho, 2005 ke aakhir mein tumne Reliance mein ₹10,000 lagaye hote. Bonus aur splits adjust karke us waqt ek share ka comparable price lagbhag ₹80.97 tha.

Ab ₹10,000 ko ₹80.97 se divide karo. Aaj ke share structure mein ye lagbhag 123.5 share-equivalent units bante.

2015 mein adjusted price ₹227.26 hui. Wahi investment? Kareeb ₹28,067.

2008 mein market ke tez girne par bahut log ghabra jaate.

2025 ke end mein adjusted price ₹1,570.40 thi. Matlab portfolio lagbhag ₹1,93,948, aur price-only profit kareeb ₹1,83,948!

Jab 2008 mein market gira tha, tum bechte ya hold karte? Comment karo. Aur research ke baad shares mein invest karna ho toh Trackk app se bhi kar sakte ho."

## Exact mathematics for the edit

These are **split/bonus-adjusted equivalent prices**, not necessarily the raw trading price per old share in 2005. Therefore `₹10,000 ÷ adjusted price` gives **fractional share-equivalent units**, *not actual shares purchased in 2005*. This normalization is what permits cross-year comparisons without separately rebuilding every historical share allotment.

| Calculation | Result |
|---|---:|
| Year-end 2005 adjusted comparable price | ₹80.97 |
| ₹10,000 ÷ ₹80.97 | 123.5025 equivalent units |
| Year-end 2015 adjusted comparable price | ₹227.26 |
| 2015 value: 123.5025 × ₹227.26 | ₹28,067 (rounded) |
| Year-end 2025 adjusted comparable price | ₹1,570.40 |
| 2025 value: 123.5025 × ₹1,570.40 | ₹1,93,948 (rounded) |
| Approx. gain: 2025 value − ₹10,000 | **₹1,83,948** |
| Price-only growth multiple | 19.39× |

**Critical:** In the on-screen formula, use full precision internally and round only the display. Do not multiply the rounded 123.5 units to claim an exact total. Price-only changes exclude dividends, tax, brokerage, rights subscriptions and demerged holdings. For Reliance, corporate actions may affect actual investor experience; this is an illustration, not a reconstructed account statement. Historical growth is not a forecast.
